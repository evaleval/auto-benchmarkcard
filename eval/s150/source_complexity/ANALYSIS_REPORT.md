# Exploratory source-complexity analysis

This analysis was specified before the outcome join. It is descriptive, non-causal, and limited to the finite 530-card collection represented by the stratified 150-card sample.

## Primary result

- Sample: 150 cards; 70 cards with at least one screen-detected, verifier-confirmed material finding.
- Kish weight-only effective sample size: 126.9.
- Weighted exposure quartiles: 1 versus 2 documentary source channels.
- Standardized weighted probabilities: 44.7% versus 54.2%.
- Risk difference: 9.5% (95% CI 1.9% to 17.1%).
- Odds ratio per unit increase in log2(1 + channel count): 1.91 (95% CI 1.13 to 3.23; p=0.015).

## Weighted descriptive probabilities by available documentary channels

- 0: n=23, 26.6% (95% CI 9.2% to 43.9%).
- 1: n=49, 50.6% (95% CI 37.2% to 63.9%).
- 2: n=63, 47.5% (95% CI 35.7% to 59.3%).
- 3_or_4: n=15, 75.5% (95% CI 55.7% to 95.4%).

## Robustness of the source-volume interpretation

- The observed one-versus-two-channel risk difference was -3.1% (95% CI -20.8% to 14.7%).
- After conditioning on complete-card fullness as a post-hoc opportunity diagnostic, the modeled one-to-two-channel risk difference was 3.9% (95% CI -7.3% to 15.1%).
- In the exact 23-field judge frame, the source-count odds ratio for unsupported content per filled field was 0.91 (95% CI 0.43 to 1.94).
- For confirmed wrong-section-splice findings per filled complete-card field, the opportunity-normalized incidence-rate ratio was 1.33 (95% CI 0.72 to 2.49).
- The planned paper-cap exclusion sensitivity is disabled after statistical audit because subsetting cards before Taylor variance estimation did not preserve the original sampling design.

## Interpretation guard

The positive prespecified log-linear trend is not a stable monotonic one-source-to-two-source contrast. Much of the endpoint pattern also coincides with card fullness, which is itself produced by the pipeline. Accordingly, the analysis does not establish that adding sources makes generation harder. The outcome records detected-and-confirmed findings, not true defect prevalence. Source count may also affect the screen's opportunity or ability to detect discrepancies. The count denotes available source types, not necessarily unique documents. The length analysis is only a budgeted-character sensitivity because nearly all full papers reached the extraction cap and exact retrieved passages were not persisted.
